About Sparvanta

Sparvanta is an iOS app developed by MR Digital for discovering online deals, browsing relevant products and configuring personalised price alerts. The app is currently in TestFlight testing and is not yet publicly available in the App Store. Retailer data feeds and affiliate links will be integrated for public use only where the required partner approvals are in place.

For publisher partnerships and enquiries: mr-digital@gmx.net.
